# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 22, 2026**

## We do not collect your data

**Nami does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information on our servers. We have no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.**

Your hydration logs and app settings stay on your device unless you choose optional features below.

## What stays on your device

Everything you put in Nami is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it.

## Optional features (your choice)

**iCloud Sync** — If you turn this on, your data syncs to your private iCloud account through Apple. It goes to your iCloud, not to us.

**Apple Health** — If you connect Apple Health, Nami can read and write water and hydration data with the Health app on your device. We do not receive this data.

You can disable or revoke these at any time in Settings or in the Health app.

## Purchases

If you buy a subscription or a lifetime plan, payment is processed by Apple. We use a payment service only to verify your purchase status. We do not receive your payment details or health data.
